# StarIS captions for SPHEREx false color

General caption (use on the viewer or color key):

False color: SPHEREx sees infrared light, invisible to the eye. Blue = shorter infrared, red = longer.

One-line caption per tag:

- blue/hot: Brighter at shorter infrared wavelengths, the pattern of a hotter star.
- neutral: Similar brightness across short, mid, and long infrared, a middling color.
- red/cool: Brighter at longer infrared wavelengths, the pattern of a cooler star.
- dusty/IR excess: The longest infrared light is brighter than a smooth stellar slope from the short infrared would predict, a sign of warm dust.
- insufficient data: Not enough good measurements in all three infrared groups to assign a color.
